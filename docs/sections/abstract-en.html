Abstract

This ontology provides a semantic model of high-risk medical devices and in vitro diagnostics within the European MDR (EU 2017/745) and IVDR (EU 2017/746) regulatory frameworks. It represents key entities such as devices, manufacturers, risks, incidents and field safety corrective actions (FSCA), enabling explicit relationships and supporting interoperability between heterogeneous systems. The model is designed to facilitate structured queries, traceability and reasoning over regulatory data.
