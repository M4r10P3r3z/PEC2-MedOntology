Introduction back to ToC

The management of medical device information in Europe involves multiple stakeholders, including manufacturers, regulatory authorities and healthcare providers. The increasing complexity of this domain requires interoperable and semantically consistent data representations. Ontologies offer a suitable solution by enabling explicit modelling of entities and relationships, as well as supporting inference and knowledge integration. This ontology has been developed to represent the European catalogue of high-risk medical devices and in vitro diagnostics. It models the relationships between devices, manufacturers, risks, reported incidents and corrective actions, following the structure of the EUDAMED datasets. In addition, controlled vocabularies have been incorporated using SKOS, and links to external resources such as DBpedia have been included to enhance interoperability.
